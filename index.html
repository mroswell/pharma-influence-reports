A four-part investigation into money, medicine & evidence

Follow the money & mind the evidence.

Four connected reports examine how pharmaceutical money moves through politics in the United States and Canada — and a fifth asks a harder question about the COVID-19 vaccines: not whether they worked, but whether the evidence needed to judge their safety was ever properly collected.

How to read this

An archive, not an argument.

These reports are presented as an evidence-based reference. They draw on peer-reviewed literature, official regulatory documents, court records, and non-partisan campaign-finance data — not on social-media claims or political commentary. Where the record is uncertain, that uncertainty is kept intact.

Important notice

This material is for informational purposes only and does not constitute medical, legal, or political advice. Nothing here recommends for or against any vaccine. Readers should consult their own physician before making decisions about vaccination or treatment. Campaign-finance correlations show patterns of giving and voting — they do not, by themselves, prove that any contribution caused any vote.
